# Similarity and recommendation: cosine similarity

!!! guiding "The question this chapter answers"
    How does a system decide two things are alike — and what does "more like this" do to the person receiving it?

!!! note "🚧 Work in progress"
    This chapter is still being written. The teaching week it belongs to is planned; the prose isn't here yet. It lands before the week it is needed.

**What it will cover.** Cosine similarity measures alignment rather than distance, which turns out to be the right question for tastes, documents and profiles. Retrieval by nearest neighbours, and a small content-based recommender you can argue with. KNN, clustering and cosine as one idea seen three ways — and the recommender as a feedback loop with a friendly face.
