# Classification by similarity: k-nearest neighbours

!!! guiding "The question this chapter answers"
    What does it mean for two things to be alike — and what happens when a system decides you are like someone else?

!!! note "🚧 Work in progress"
    This chapter is still being written. The teaching week it belongs to is planned; the prose isn't here yet. It lands before the week it is needed.

**What it will cover.** Judging by company rather than by rules: nearest neighbours vote. Why scaling and the choice of distance decide who your neighbours are, and so where the judgement actually lives. Train/test splits and why testing on your training data is meaningless. A recommendation and a stereotype wear the same coat; the difference is which features someone decided counted as similarity.
