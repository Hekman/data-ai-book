# Groups without labels: k-means clustering

!!! guiding "The question this chapter answers"
    How do systems group people without labels — and what does it mean to be sorted into a category you never chose?

!!! note "🚧 Work in progress"
    This chapter is still being written. The teaching week it belongs to is planned; the prose isn't here yet. It lands before the week it is needed.

**What it will cover.** Unsupervised learning: k-means proposes groups where nobody supplied an answer to check against. Choosing k with reasoning rather than by eye, profiling and naming clusters, and recognising a clustering that is arbitrary or unstable. Segmentation as a quiet, consequential act of authorship — consent, stereotyping, differential treatment. Teardown: Spotify Wrapped beside a platform's ad-preferences page.
